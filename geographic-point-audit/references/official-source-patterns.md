# Official source patterns

## 1. Park acceptance lists

A district page may say “N parks passed municipal acceptance” and name only a subset.

Required record:

- exact total claimed by the source;
- exact named subset;
- date and issuing authority;
- any dimensions or facilities explicitly stated;
- explicit blanks for free admission, hours, and current equipment when the source omits them.

Never reconstruct the unnamed remainder from map entries or unrelated batches.

## 2. New parks and playgrounds

Search for the project owner, district construction announcement, operator page, and follow-up opening notice.

A project announcement may use “about to open,” while a later notice says “now open.” Use the latest dated official status. Facility completion does not prove every interactive device is operational; retain a field-verification item.

## 3. Venues in transition

Check both old and new sites. If the old site remains open, write the old entrance as current and the new site as future. Count the institution once unless the old site has closed and the new site is independently operating.

## 4. Child-friendly labels

Keep these distinct unless an official source explicitly links them:

- `儿童友好公园` (park-space acceptance);
- `儿童友好生活场景` (broader venue/service scene);
- `儿童友好社区/街区` (community or block program);
- `国家/省级旅游景区` (tourism rating).

Record the exact issuer, date, and label. When a later government article contradicts an earlier complete list, preserve the conflict and avoid creating a synthetic merged list.

## 5. Commercial venues

Government confirmation can establish that a branch is in a mall, but not necessarily its current floor, unit, price, or daily hours. Use operator contact or on-site evidence for dynamic fields.

## 6. Water and sports activities

Official project pages often prove scale, opening, and event capability, not that every activity is available to every age every day. Separate:

- permanent infrastructure;
- scheduled competitions;
- public booking or classes;
- age limits and safety requirements;
- prices and cancellation rules.

Do not generalize a one-off event to a permanent age rule.
